# Prism Plus prototype feedback

Thank you for trying Prism Plus. Please include your Mac model and macOS version, then tell us:

1. Could you install and open the app without help?
2. Could you create or open a `.tex` file and compile it?
3. Did autocomplete, diagnostics, formatting, outline navigation, and PDF preview feel clear?
4. Did any action blink, freeze, lose text, or behave differently from what you expected?
5. What is the single most important improvement you would make?

For a bug, please include the exact steps, what you expected, what happened, and a screenshot.
